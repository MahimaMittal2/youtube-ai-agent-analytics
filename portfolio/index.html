STEP 1 · CONTROLLED EXPERIMENTATION

Randomized A/B Test Scorecard

In a 60-day stratified randomized controlled trial (N = 19,404), AI Agent V2 delivered a statistically significant +9.85 pp lift in SARR and cut hallucination error rates by 30.20%.

STEP 2 · METRIC INNOVATION

The False Containment Wedge

Headline containment creates an optical illusion of efficiency; true SARR resolution exposes an operational gap widening to over 17 pp in complex policy and copyright domains.

STEP 3 · SUBGROUP SEGMENTATION

Heterogeneous Treatment Effects by Complexity

Low complexity captured strong SARR gains (+15.63 pp), while High complexity suffered from aggressive over-containment and a wider false containment gap (26.2 pp).

STEP 4 · AI QUALITY & BEHAVIOR

Sentiment Penalty and Repeat Failure Demand

AI factual and policy errors are associated with a -56.0% decline in Mean CSAT (4.52 → 1.99 stars) and drive a 4.26× surge in 7-day repeat contact failure demand.

STEP 5 · WORKFORCE CAPACITY

Containment Sensitivity & Diminishing Returns

Pushing containment beyond ~80% triggers a +55.1% spike in repeat failure demand, reversing workforce savings and increasing total human labor hours from 2,071 to 2,111 hrs/month.

EXECUTIVE RECOMMENDATION

Recommended Strategy: Selective Rollout (Option B)

“Selective rollout outperformed a universal deployment strategy in the simulated analysis: automate low- and medium-complexity workflows broadly while routing high-complexity cases to human specialists.”

1. Deploy V2 for Low & Medium Complexity: Captures ~94% of observed incremental SARR gains across 80% of creator support volume.
2. Intelligent Early Triage for High Complexity: Bypasses the 26.2 pp false-containment trap by routing policy disputes directly to human specialists.
3. Modernize KPI Governance: Replaces isolated containment targets with SARR and Quality-Adjusted Containment (QAC) launch gates.
